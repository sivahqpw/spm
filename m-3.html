Meditation Product Step#3

Lean Architecture Prompt

Lean Product Structure (Mandatory)

This product must not be unnecessarily long, because customers are paying "£17–£47" to achieve a practical, meaningful quick win—not to complete a mini course.

Every page should help the customer take the next action immediately.

Ideal Product Structure

"PDF (20–30 pages)"
↓
"6 Worksheets"
↓
"6 Checklists"
↓
"10–15 AI Prompts"
↓
"Final Action Plan"

---

MANDATORY RULE

"If an asset does not help the customer take action immediately, it does not belong in the product."
"Every asset must produce one tangible output that the customer can immediately use."

Examples of tangible outputs include:

* A completed worksheet
* A finished checklist
* A written decision
* A personalized action plan
* A completed meditation session
* A completed reflection
* A daily practice schedule
* A next-step commitment

If an asset only provides information without leading to an immediate action or usable output, it should be removed or redesigned.
